Top 5 MLB Players of All Time

Read more on ESPN
Name Position(s) Teams What he's best known for
Babe Ruth Right Field/Left Field Boston Red Sox, New York Yankees, Boston Braves Home runs. The baseball we watch today is Babe Ruth's game. Many players make an impact, a few become folk heroes, but nobody changed a sport like Ruth did when he joined the Yankees and transformed baseball into a game of power. No player dominated his era like Ruth.
Willie Mays Center Field Birmingham Black Barons, New York/San Francisco Giants, New York Mets Known for "The Catch" in the 1954 World Series. Mays combined athleticism, grace, and power and became one of the greatest center fielders ever.
Hank Aaron Right Field Indianapolis Clowns, Milwaukee/Atlanta Braves, Milwaukee Brewers Famous for breaking Babe Ruth’s home run record with No. 715. Aaron was one of the most consistent hitters in baseball history.
Ty Cobb Center Field Detroit Tigers, Philadelphia Athletics One of the greatest hitters ever. Cobb won 12 batting titles and finished with the highest lifetime batting average in MLB history.
Ted Williams Left Field Boston Red Sox Williams was one of the greatest hitters who ever lived and the last player to hit .400 in a season.
